The five rules that decide the event

Everything in the manual is detail. These five rules are the event.

Rule 1 — only the Garrison Leader gets the time The Palace timer only counts for the Defence Garrison Leader holding the tile. Everyone else in the Palace gets points and rewards. They get 0 seconds of Palace time. The goal is not to lead many rallies — it is to install one named player and keep him there.
Rule 2 — buffs beat bodies An Aerie buff gives your whole alliance +50% to one stat for 30 minutes. Two Aeries held is worth more than 40% more troops. Do not rush the Palace with no buffs active — take the towers first.
Rule 3 — buffs stay in your alliance An Aerie buff or a Crystal Skill only reaches your own alliance. A partner alliance holding a tower does not buff you, even if you are coordinating with them. Build your own crystal supply.
Rule 4 — where troops die Fighting for the Palace or an Aerie is safe. Attacking a player's town is not. Palace and Aerie fights only injure — nobody dies. Attacking a town kills troops for good. Your infirmary is unlimited only on the battlefield.
Rule 5 — heal before you leave When you leave the battlefield, your infirmary goes back to its normal size. Every injured soldier above that size dies. This kills more troops than the enemy does. Heal down before you exit.
